HOW TO PLAY IN 3D

Modern 2026 Fluid 3D Action RPG Mechanics

Movement:
• W A S D: Smooth continuous 3D movement with full wall-sliding physics.
• In Action RPG view: A moves & turns Left, D moves & turns Right.
• In 3rd Person / 1st Person: Press T or click 🕹 A/D in header to toggle between:
  • Strafe Mode: A/D strafes Left/Right, Q/E or Arrow keys turn.
  • Turn Mode: A/D turns camera Left/Right, Q/E strafes.

Mouse & Camera Controls:
• Left-Click: Attack / Trigger Slash / Spellcast in real-time.
• Right-Click Drag: Orbit 3D Camera 360° and adjust vertical tilt/pitch around your hero!
• Mouse Wheel (Scroll): Zoom 3D Camera In / Out (0.6x close up to 2.2x wide tactical overview).
• U or click 🖱️ Mouse Look: Lock the pointer for authentic first-person & third-person mouse aiming & turning (Press ESC to unlock).
• V or click 🎥 Camera: Switch between Action RPG 3D, 3rd Person 3D, and 1st Person 3D.

Audio & Music:
• Click 🎵 Music: Toggle atmospheric dark dungeon synth background soundtrack [BGM].
• Click 🔊 SFX: Toggle retro 16-bit sound effects.

Modern Holographic Map:
• Press M or click 🗺 Map [M]: Open the Tactical Reconnaissance Scanner with full subterranean topography, zoom/pan controls, exploration progress, and live telemetry!
• In-Game Circular Radar HUD: Floating glassmorphic radar in the top-right corner with 360° radar sweep, player vision cone, compass bearing, pulsing enemy blips, and loot markers.
• Use + / − on the radar or tactical map to adjust zoom scale.

Goal: Delve down through Floor 1 to Floor 4 and defeat Malakor the Arch-Lich!
